The Business Problem

Funnel Drop-off Challenge

In modern e-commerce, digital marketing campaigns drive massive quantities of visitor traffic, yet typically only 10% to 16% of browsing sessions result in an actual financial purchase.

Without automated session qualification, marketing spend is squandered uniformly across all visitors. Businesses face two severe costs:

  • False Negatives (Missed Revenue): An interested visitor hesitates on checkout and leaves because no personalized incentive was offered in time.
  • False Positives (Wasted Margin): Costly discount codes are shown to visitors who would have purchased at full price anyway, eroding profit margins.

ConvertSense AI calculates calibrated purchase probabilities during active visitor sessions, empowering CRM systems to trigger high-impact interventions precisely when needed.

Methodological Rigor & Target Leakage Prevention

Data Science Best Practice
Why PageValues is Excluded: In Google Analytics, the PageValues metric averages transaction revenues backwards across pages visited. Including it artificially inflates test accuracy (AUC > 0.90) because it carries post-transaction ground truth!

To build a genuine pre-purchase production pipeline, we enforce strict isolation:

Stratified Split (80/20) Preserves exact 15.5% purchase minority proportion across train and test sets.
Deduplication 125 identical sessions removed to prevent duplicate records appearing across splits.
Categorical Safety OperatingSystems, Browser, Region & TrafficType treated categorically via One-Hot Encoding.
Configurable Cutoff Tunable decision threshold (default 0.40) optimizes recall over pure accuracy.
